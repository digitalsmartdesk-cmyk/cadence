Notes

Form
A stylised C derived from the Cormorant Garamond letterform. The 192 version carries asymmetric stress (heavier left bow, thinner top/bottom) and small beak terminals at the aperture — a quiet nod to the serif's open curl.
Scale
The 32 version drops the beaks and uses a uniform stroke arc. At ≤24 px the serif details would blur into the bowl; the geometric form holds shape down to 16.
Aperture
Opening angle ≈ 80°. Wider than a standard C, which keeps the inner counter visible at small sizes and prevents the mark from reading as a solid disc.
